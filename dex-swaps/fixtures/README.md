# Swap regression fixtures

These protobuf-encoded `sf.solana.type.v1.ConfirmedTransaction` fixtures are derived from unmodified public Solana `getTransaction` results (JSON encoding). They retain signatures, resolved account metadata, instructions, inner instructions, logs, and token balances needed by the decoders. No diagnostic instruction edits or synthetic token balances are included.

`token-fees/damm-swap-referral.transaction.pb` and `damm-swap2-referral.transaction.pb` retain finalized DAMM v2 calls whose referral destination aliases the primary output account. Their owned Token-2022 mint bytes and `damm-referral-proof.json` fix the original CPI order, gross amounts, fee epoch, net primary receipt and balance evidence. The core regression test verifies that only the primary net receipt contributes to the swap and that the referral never substitutes for a zero primary output. The official program revision is pinned in the proof and validation documentation.

`native-venues/fluxbeam-rounded-input-1/2.transaction.pb` retain two finalized Fluxbeam swaps whose exact input debits are below the requested amount. `fluxbeam-input-proof.json` retains the original instruction and balance evidence. The core test verifies their actual debit/receipt pairs and rejects failed bounds or changed declared account roles; no fee metadata is needed to prove these two receipts by conservation.

`token-fees/dbc-burned-closed-output-1/2.transaction.pb` retain finalized DBC transfer-hook outputs subsequently burned and closed. `closed-output-proof.json` retains the native instruction, pre/post balances and byte hashes. The core test proves each receipt from its observed starting balance, burn and committed close, and rejects incomplete or contradictory lifecycle evidence.

DefiTuna FusionAMM fixtures `swap-coverage/129..166.transaction.pb` are original finalized Firehose transactions recovered from the deployed candidate ledger. The owner-verified on-chain IDL (FusionAMM 1.1.4) fixes the swap discriminator, direction, exact-input/output fields, pool, user, mint and vault accounts. Independent inspection verifies 38 swap legs across eight program-owned pools, both directions, optional supplemental-tick slices and Token-2022 receipts. `swap-coverage/defituna-evidence.json` records source hashes, pool owners and exact financial identities. Two-hop calls still require their own per-pool decoder and real fixtures.

Invariant fixtures `swap-coverage/167..206.transaction.pb` independently verify 40 finalized swap legs in two program-owned pools, both directions, including temporary WSOL accounts. The published contract fixes the exact-input/output layout and optional referral debit. The financial unit test separately verifies referral accounting and rejects changed amounts, undeclared recipients and additional debits; no referral was present in this real sample. `swap-coverage/invariant-evidence.json` records source revision, hashes, owners and exact amounts.

`phoenix-immediate-1/2.transaction.pb` preserve finalized swaps at slots 455604700 and 455598049. Phoenix swap/marketable-order candidates are retained even without wallet transfers; post-only placement, cancel, deposit and withdrawal are excluded from this intent classification. The diagnostic is not a normalized fill or price. The test separately simulates an absent transfer trace to verify retention; full pricing still needs event quantities and authoritative market lot sizes.

`swap-coverage/pump-delegated-1..5.transaction.pb` retain five finalized PumpSwap multihop parents which delegate all seven physical legs to Pump bonding-curve child calls. `pump-delegated-proof.json` records their original hashes, scope indices and exact amounts. The diagnostic suppresses a parent only when every well-formed declared hop has a distinct, positive, transfer-verified physical leg in that invocation, matching its pool and directed mints. Missing, malformed, mismatched and reused evidence remains replayable. No new financial rows are produced by this diagnostic correction.

`token-fees/stabble-dlmm-shared-output.transaction.pb` retains one finalized Stabble V2 nested vault withdrawal followed by DLMM Swap2. Both outputs credit one user account. The independently owner/epoch-verified `stabble-shared-output-mint.bin` and `stabble-shared-output-proof.json` fix each Token-2022 transfer's 300-bps fee and its separate primary receipt. Neither the aggregate balance delta nor Stabble's separate fee-destination receipt substitutes for those outputs. The core test rejects absent/inconsistent fee metadata and an undeclared nested vault program while preserving the independent DLMM leg.
